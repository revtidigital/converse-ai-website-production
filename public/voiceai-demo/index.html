How This Call Comes Together

Aurelia Motors runs Carol on two sides of the same conversation: calls that come in directly, and callbacks that go out the moment someone fills a form on the website.

Inbound lane

Customer calls in

Someone calls in wanting a test drive. Carol picks up, figures out what they need, and either gets them booked or passes it along for a dealer follow-up.

Outbound lane

Website form triggers a call-back

Someone fills out the test-drive request form on the site. That submission flows straight to Carol, who calls back already knowing what they asked for.

A Few Calls, Start to Finish

The full outbound callback, as recorded
CarolHi, this is Carol from Aurelia Motors. Am I speaking with Sophia?
SophiaHello. Yeah, I'm Sophia.
CarolI'm reaching out about your interest in the Voltis Limited, for pickup at Aurelia Motors Denver, Colorado. Shall we go ahead with that?
SophiaCan you tell me what's the difference between Aero EV and Voltis EV?
CarolThe Voltis is a larger electric sedan with a dual motor and 420 horsepower, while the Aero EV is a more compact electric hatchback with a single motor and 210 horsepower.
SophiaIf I drive about 50 miles every day in Denver city, which car is better?
CarolBoth would easily handle a 50-mile daily commute. Aero EV is compact for parking and city traffic; Voltis offers more space and power.
SophiaWhat's the range difference?
CarolVoltis has 410 miles of range, while Aero EV has 290 miles of range.
SophiaAnd what's the price difference?
CarolVoltis Limited is $57,495 MSRP. Aero EV Limited is $38,295 MSRP.
SophiaWhat are the finance options if I go with Aero EV?
CarolFinancing terms range from 36 to 72 months. Approximate APR ranges from 4.9% to 18.9% depending on credit tier. Leasing may also be available.
SophiaI think I'm more interested in Aero EV. Can you book a test drive?
CarolAbsolutely. Is this your first car, or are you adding another vehicle?
SophiaIt's my first car.
CarolDo you have access to home charging, like a garage or driveway with an outlet?
SophiaYes, I have it.
CarolI have your ZIP code as 80204. Is that right?
SophiaYes, correct.
CarolWhat day works best for your test drive?
SophiaNext Sunday.
CarolSo that's Sunday, August 16, 2026 — does that work?
SophiaLet's do it 10 AM in the morning.
CarolI've booked your test drive for the Aero EV at Aurelia Motors Denver on Sunday, August 16, 2026 at 10 AM.

Common pushback

Questions People Ask, and Honest Answers

Question

"Isn't this just a robocaller?"

Answer: A robocaller reads a script. Carol asks real questions, answers real ones back, adapts when the customer changes their mind, and gets to a booked appointment.

Question

"Can it really handle detailed product questions?"

Answer: Within what it's been given, yes — in this call alone Carol covers range, pricing, financing, down payments, and EV incentives across two different vehicles.

Question

"What if the customer changes their mind mid-call?"

Answer: That's exactly what happens in the recording — Sophia moves from the Voltis Limited to the Aero EV, and Carol just carries the conversation forward.

Question

"What about saying something it shouldn't?"

Answer: Carol sticks to ranges on finance and incentive questions, and hands off exact numbers to the dealership's finance team.

After the walkthrough

Questions Worth Asking Next

Use these right after showing the recording to figure out where the current process actually breaks down.

How leads move today
  • How many calls and web leads come in on an average day?
  • How long does it usually take to call someone back?
  • What share of leads get a callback within five minutes?
  • How many leads just never get followed up on?
  • What's the current test-drive booking rate?
What buyers actually ask
  • What do buyers usually ask before booking a test drive?
  • Do they often compare more than one model on the same call?
  • What usually stalls a booking — price, range, financing, timing, or distance?
  • Do people prefer a call, a text, or an email follow-up?
  • What's the current process for someone who's not ready yet?
Systems already in place
  • Where do web leads land today — which CRM or system?
  • Can the website form send data to an external system?
  • Where do dealer locations and open test-drive slots live?
  • What has to be known before a test drive can be booked?
  • How is lead source and outcome tracked right now?
What a pilot would look like
  • Which model or campaign makes sense to start with?
  • Start with inbound, outbound, or both at once?
  • What numbers can we use as a baseline?
  • Who owns lead routing and follow-up today?
  • What result would justify rolling this out further?
